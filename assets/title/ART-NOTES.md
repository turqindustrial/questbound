# Title art

## The emblem

`questbound-seal-source.webp` (1254 × 1212) is the host's own art, supplied on 2026-10-10 with the words "make this the logo and title screen and animate it": on a dark plum-black ground, a crimson scaled serpent with a dragon's head (at the top, facing right) bites its tail round a large faceted crimson Q whose tail is a dagger; the blade crosses the ring towards the lower right. Four-point stars sit on the ring at west, east and south (the north star crowns the Q), with a sun and moons on a thin inner circle. It replaces the drawn serpent (`serpent.js`, retired) and the lettered Q seal everywhere: the launch card, the title page, the story-loading seal, the top bar, the boot card, the pairing page and the app icons. It is the host's, not generated by the game.

`node make-emblem.cjs` (headless Chrome or Edge, no internet) prepares it. The square is cut round the ring's centre (622, 588 in the source; the serpent's outer edge is 520 from it, its inner edge 451, the head and the south star reach 612). The dark ground becomes transparency by colour to alpha against the painting's own background (20, 1, 15), so on the game's dark backdrop the emblem looks as painted. The picture is then split:

- `public/emblem/ring-v1.webp`: everything beyond the inner circle, and the parts of the ring stars and of the dragon's jaw that reach in across it. Under the dagger's blade this layer holds scales from two scale rows further round the ring (a row every 7.1°), so when it turns nothing of the blade goes with it.
- `public/emblem/core-v1.webp`: the Q, the inner circle with its sun, moons and dots, and the whole blade (its edges measured from the painting: `y = x − 39` and `y = 499.5 + x/2` meeting at the point, 1077, 1038), taken with its dark outline; over the serpent the blade is kept exactly as painted (a shadow darker than the ground cannot be made of transparency).
- Two small blurred copies for the breathing glow, `seal-v1.webp` (264 px, the whole emblem: boot card, pairing page, top bar) and `questbound-emblem.webp` here (the whole emblem for the app icons and native builds).

The game turns the ring once every 90 s, clockwise, the way the dragon faces, under the still Q and blade; the glow breathes, a light sweeps over the Q and the stars twinkle (webTheme.js `[data-qb=emblem]`). The Light atmosphere, reduced motion and full-screen covers hold it still. The colour themes tint it with a CSS filter (themeRules.js `emblem`). When the painting changes, run `node make-emblem.cjs`, then `node make-icons.cjs`, and raise `-vN` in the file names and `?v=N` in the icon links.

## The title painting

`questbound-title.jpg` (1536 × 1024) is the backdrop of the title screen and the menus. It was painted on 2026-10-02 by the game's own illustration service (`world-art.cjs`, kind `landscape`) from this description:

> A vast fantasy valley at dusk seen from a high ridge. On the right-hand third of the picture a lone cloaked traveller stands on a rocky outcrop holding a small warm lantern, seen from behind, small against the land. Beyond them a pale road winds down through dark pine woods past a stone bridge over a silver river toward a ruined round tower on a crag, and far snow mountains under towering storm-lit clouds with a break of gold light. The left-hand third of the picture is quiet, dark forested hillside in deep teal shadow with very little detail. Everything runs edge to edge: a full wide panorama with no frame, no vignette border and no empty margins.

The two battle paintings that replaced it later on 2026-10-02 (four heroes against a lich, one wide and one tall) were retired on 2026-10-04 at the host's request, and this painting came back. They remain in the repository's history.

### How it is shown (`App.js`, `HomeScreen.js` `homeLayout`, `webTheme.js`)

- The picture always covers the window (`resizeMode="cover"`; `s.backdrop` sets width and height to 100%). An image loaded from a file carries its own pixel size as its style, and without that the picture once stopped short and left wide windows black on the right.
- Wide screens and a phone on its side (`data-frame=wide`, from `homeLayout(width,height).wide`): the view sits at 50% 42%, the horizon. The left third is dark on purpose: the name and the menu sit there (`atmosphere-home` darkens it a little more). The saved hero is a slim strip in the bottom right corner, below the lantern-bearer's feet (on a 1366 × 768 laptop the strip is at about y 650 to 712, the traveller's feet at about y 562).
- An upright phone (`data-frame=tall`): only a strip of the picture fits, so the view moves to 62% 50%, the tower, the bridge and the peak. The name sits above, the hero strip and the compact menu below (`atmosphere-home-narrow` darkens the bottom for them).
- The painting drifts in only a little (`qb-drift`, scale 1 to 1.05: the host asked for it zoomed out on 2026-10-02) and holds still in the Light atmosphere.

## The app icon

The app icons are the page `icon.html` in this folder: the emblem (`questbound-emblem.webp`) on the game's dark launch glow, drawn offline. `node make-icons.cjs` photographs it at each size with a headless Chrome or Edge (home screen, manifest, browser tab and the Android adaptive layers). The 192, 180 and 48 px icons are cut close to the serpent ring (`simple`) so the Q stays readable; the monochrome layer is the emblem's silhouette. After changing the icon, raise `?v=N` in the icon links (`public/manifest.json`, `public/index.html`, `fullscreen.js`, the pairing page in `phone-server.cjs`; `?v=4` since 2026-10-10) so installed copies and browser tabs fetch the new one. An iPhone keeps the icon it was added with: remove the home-screen icon and add it again.
