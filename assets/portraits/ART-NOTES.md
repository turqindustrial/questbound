# Conversation portraits

Created with the built-in Image Generation tool. Original assets are copied into this project as keeper-v1.png and mara-v1.png. These are the two current portrait archetypes; generated adventures reuse them for their two starting NPCs. Older stories keep their established lore.

Saved assets: [Keeper portrait](keeper-v1.png) and [Mara portrait](mara-v1.png).

## Keeper prompt

Create a single square fantasy RPG dialogue portrait, close head-and-shoulders of a weathered middle-aged human man, short salt-and-pepper beard, brown hair, expressive kind but firm eyes, plain linen shirt with dark russet waistcoat, warm amber candle light and dark defocused inn interior. Painterly realistic oil illustration, sophisticated muted gold and deep blue palette, highly legible face at small sizes, straight on slight three-quarter angle, centered with generous headroom. NO text, NO border, NO UI. This is an original modest townsfolk innkeeper, not a famous character. Produce one portrait image only.

## Mara prompt

Create a single square fantasy RPG dialogue portrait, close head-and-shoulders of a young adult human woman with warm brown skin, dark curly hair loosely braided, thoughtful lively eyes, practical teal traveling cloak over a plain blouse, small leather satchel strap, warm side lighting in a dark defocused tavern interior. Painterly realistic oil illustration, sophisticated muted gold and deep blue palette, highly legible face at small sizes, straight on slight three-quarter angle, centered with generous headroom. NO text, NO border, NO UI. Original traveling medicine courier, not a famous character. Produce one portrait image only.
