## Implemented progression

Creation still starts at level 1. Each victory offers one earned level, up to level 20; there is no player-controlled level selector. Level-up reviews HP, proficiency, ability-score increases at standard ASI levels, subclass choice at 3, prepared spells, Wizard spellbooks, and Warlock Mystic Arcanum. A confirmed level-up saves the character and begins a rested encounter at the new level. The custom encounter scales its HP, AC, attack bonus, and damage with level.

HP uses the fixed average after first level and applies Constitution, Tough, and Dwarf bonuses per level. Proficiency, weapon bonuses, Monk damage dice, Second Wind, Lay on Hands, and basic Extra Attack progression scale. Some supported subclass benefits are applied: Champion weapon critical ranges, Draconic HP/unarmored defense, Life healing boosts, Fiend temporary HP, four SRD subclass spell tables, and certain subclass Extra Attacks. No class has every level 1–20 feature fully automated.

229 level-gated SRD feature references are available in the sheet. A reference description does not grant an automated effect. Missing features are explicitly marked. Most of the selected subclasses are outside SRD and do not yet have their complete feature implementation. Eldritch Knight and Arcane Trickster spellcasting, additional subclass spell tables, companions, maneuvers, auras, transformations, most reactions, optional feats, Epic Boons, and capstone features remain to be implemented. Artificer base progression still uses the previously documented playtest compatibility mapping.

The full published class chapters are needed to verify non-SRD mechanics before implementing them. The official Handbook chapter redirected to its marketplace page; Artificer and Forgotten Realms indexes provided headings/overviews rather than the full feature rules in the material inspected. Relevant books: revised Player's Handbook, Eberron: Forge of the Artificer, and Forgotten Realms: Heroes of Faerûn. No purchases or sign-ins were performed.

## Turns and rests (2026-10-01)

A turn in a creature fight is one action and one bonus action. Bonus actions: a healing draught (2024 rules), Second Wind, Lay on Hands, a Monk's Martial Arts strike and bonus-action spells. After the action the turn waits for a bonus action or End turn whenever one is worth offering. Short rests follow Baldur's Gate 3: two between long rests (three for a Bard of level 2 or more), each restoring half the hero's and each following companion's hit points, Second Wind and a Warlock's slots. A long rest restores hit points, slots, features and short rests and leaves the rest of the adventure untouched. Not yet covered: Action Surge, hit dice, reactions on a foe's turn, and bonus actions in brawls with people (those keep the simpler one-move turn with Wait).

## Fight balance (2026-10-01)

`node balance-check.cjs` simulates every ready-made hero at levels 1, 3 and 5 against every creature template with a plain tactic (main weapon or the strongest simple spell, a draught or a heal when low) and flags fights under 35% or over 97% wins. The creature curve in `foeStatsFor` was softened from it: HP grows 45% per level (30% for packs, whose every member attacks) instead of 80%, and attack and damage bonuses rise every third level instead of every second. Before, a level-3 rogue beat a bandit 63% of the time and a level-5 rogue 24%; now 94% and 87%. Known gap: Sneak Attack is not implemented, so rogues stay weakest against the orc and packs. Heroes under 12 HP at levels 1–2 are never given a pack or the orc in the Random hostile encounter.

## Spell casting and verification

All 339 catalog spells have a casting route in the rules layer: 24 automatic single-encounter effects and 315 visible DM-ruling routes. The latter includes fixed-rule spells whose automation is unfinished, not just inherently narrative spells. A DM route passing its test does NOT mean the spell's individual mechanics are automated or fully verified.

Automatic spells: Acid Splash, Sacred Flame, Vicious Mockery, Shocking Grasp, Eldritch Blast, Fire Bolt, Poison Spray, Chill Touch, Ray of Frost, Burning Hands, Fireball, Lightning Bolt, Chain Lightning, Circle of Death, Scorching Ray, Inflict Wounds, Magic Missile, Cure Wounds, Healing Word, Mass Cure Wounds, Mass Healing Word, and False Life.

Automatic attacks apply to the current wisp and healing applies to the player. Other targets, world effects, multiple creatures, conditions not modeled in combat, and narrative outcomes require a DM ruling. The demo does not model full positioning or object ignition. Some secondary cantrip effects are recorded as state but the associated movement/reaction systems remain incomplete. Components and casting requirements require user/DM confirmation; material inventory costs are not automatically deducted.

Casting includes spell-slot choice and upcasting, cantrip scaling, shared level-by-level slot costs, bonus-action tracking, one spell-slot expenditure per player turn, rituals outside combat, Arcanum uses, concentration checks after enemy damage, and simple duration counters. Pending DM requests spend nothing; confirmation spends the cost and records the ruling plus explicit HP effects. Reaction and long-casting-time DM rulings are handled separately from ordinary automatic turns. New confirmed rulings are retained in both the 40-entry adventure log and the persistent campaign journal. Earlier rulings are not reconstructed.

`node verify-expansion.cjs` generates SPELL-COVERAGE.json and verifies all 339 dispatch/cost/save paths, independent low/high outcomes for 24 automatic effects, slot exhaustion, concentration, bonus-action timing, exactly four subclasses per class, passive level gates, and level 1–20 stat/save validity. ## Protective magic follow-up
Shield of Faith now grants +2 AC to the caster, including on the adventure screen and character sheet. Blur gives incoming attacks disadvantage except for attackers with Blindsight or Truesight. Both can be cast before combat; their bonus/action timing is respected in combat. These automatic protections are attached to concentration, so replacement, failed saves, manual ending, zero HP, and expiration remove them. Saved protections restore on reload; old or manually adjudicated concentration does not silently acquire an automated self effect.
Durations count combat rounds (100 for Shield of Faith, 10 for Blur). Travel time and intervening turns during DM-adjudicated long casting remain manual; use End concentration when the DM determines a duration has elapsed. Other targets use the existing DM ruling route. Source: bundled SRD 5.2 and https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/spell-descriptions .
