# Inn combat

Attacks and committed harmful NPC spells start an initiative encounter. Present, conscious defenders join according to authored relationships. The keeper attacks; Mara uses Help to defend the keeper and attacks if personally targeted. Explicitly recorded player allegiance supports allied defenders; no starter ally is granted automatically.

The engine resolves attacks, misses, criticals, damage, temporary HP and concentration. Downed participants stop acting. Initiative and rounds persist across reloads. Send Dodge, Wait, Flee or Surrender through the DM. Roll displays omit modifier arithmetic.

Verified with deterministic combat, spell, persistence and NPC consequence tests, web export, and a live isolated browser encounter. The keeper reduced the test character from 15 to 13 HP; reload retained 13 HP and round 2. The player's main save was untouched.

Current scope is the authored inn encounter, not a general tactical grid simulation. Flee and surrender are simplified exits. Reaching 0 HP ends this encounter without death saves or capture simulation. Story prose does not grant allies or spawn arbitrary combatants.

Rules reference: https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/playing-the-game (initiative, turns, Help, Dodge, attacks and critical hits). NPC statistics and motivations are authored campaign content.
