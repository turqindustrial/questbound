# Playtest checklist (about 15 minutes)

Open the playtest link you were sent and enter the invite code. (Hosting on your own PC and Wi-Fi instead: run **Questbound.cmd**, open the Phone link it prints and enter the pairing code.)
Tick off what you can, then tell the host or [open a playtest issue](../../issues/new/choose) with what you found.

## 1. First look (3 min)
Use headphones if you have them.
- [ ] The Q emblem and QUESTBOUND title fade in, then "Tap anywhere to begin". Tap: a low swell plays and the title card fades to the main menu.
- [ ] The main menu fits on the screen upright and on its side. Buttons are easy to hit with a thumb; nothing scrolls sideways.
- [ ] Menu music is playing. ♪ (top right) opens the Master, Music, Ambience and Effects levels, Night mode and Mute.
- [ ] Tap ⛶ (top right). Android: the game fills the screen, and tapping ⛶ again leaves. iPhone: follow the Add to Home Screen steps, open the gold Q icon, pair once, and check there are no browser bars.

## 2. Combat test (5 min)
New Adventure → play your character → tap **Random hostile encounter** → **Begin the encounter** (right under the card).
- [ ] The game fills the screen with no page scrolling: your HP bar at the top, the story in the middle, the message box above the Story/Quest/Map/Log tabs.
- [ ] The foe's HP strip (HP bar, AC) sits above the story; tapping it shows everyone's HP. The Quest tab has the full nameplate; for a group, it shows how many are standing.
- [ ] Combat starts with a drum hit and the music changes to drums and brass.
- [ ] Above the message box: your weapon in gold, then Dodge, Retreat and Potion. Swipe the row for more.
- [ ] Use the action buttons to attack, dodge and drink the potion, and type a spell if you have one (Cast… starts the sentence). Dice, swings and misses sound right. With headphones, your hits land on the right (the foe) and the foe's hits on the left (you); fire, cold and lightning each sound different.
- [ ] Drop below 30% HP: the HP bar pulses red and a heartbeat starts.
- [ ] Turn the phone on its side: the story and the side column sit next to each other and still fit.
- [ ] Retreat, or get knocked out: you end up at the Caravan Camp and the captain or scout speaks first.
- [ ] Walk back and win: the victory sound plays, then the camp greets you when you return.
- [ ] After the win, a gold **Level up** action leads the row. Level up: you return to camp at full HP, and the travel actions (and the Map tab's Travel buttons) take you onward.

## 3. A story opening (4 min)
New Adventure → **A Bell Beneath the Ice** (or The Last Caravan).
- [ ] The two locals are already talking when the story opens.
- [ ] Talk to one by first name ("Sella, what happened?"). Their reply is labelled with their name; narration is labelled Dungeon Master.
- [ ] Waiting for the Dungeon Master and the "Preparing the next scene" screen feel acceptable on your connection.

## 4. Shared table (4 min)
On the PC: Multiplayer → type a name → **Start the table with my adventure**.
On the phone: Multiplayer → another name → **Join the table** → Play at the table.
- [ ] Both devices show the same adventure, and the table bar lists two players.
- [ ] Send an action on the phone: the PC shows "…is taking a turn", then plays the turn with the phone player's name on it.
- [ ] Close the browser on the phone, reopen the Phone link: it is still at the table and caught up.

## 5. Move your hero (2 min)
- [ ] Settings → **Move your hero** → Copy save code. Open the game in another browser or device, load the code there: the same hero and adventure appear.

## Tell me
Anything that felt slow, confusing, hard to tap, too quiet or loud, or broken — with the phone model and browser.
